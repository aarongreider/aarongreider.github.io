🛍️  Jungle Jim's Digital Weekly Ad

UI Design
React
Wordpress
Firebase

This is a future app to be implemented with the next iteration of junglejims.com. I took the time to explore how we could digitize our old fashioned weekly ad, which is currently hosted on the website as a pdf and a jpg (both?!) that we update week to week with new deals.

With my iteration, users would be able to search a the ad in a familiar layout, filter items, and add them to their shopping list. While Jungle Jim's will never have an ordering online option, this would replace shoppers needing to carry around our newspaper-sized ad around the store, cutting down on printing costs and time needed to update all the physical media of the ad. junglejims.com/item-search
