Below you'll find a gallery of posters, murals, logos, and other artworks from my time at Jungle Jim's. I've created visual vocabularies for more events than I can count, including logos, posters, and wayfinding graphics for new events and festivals.

Metro Bus Wraps

As part of our 50th anniversary celebration, we wanted to bring the Jungle to the city of Cincinnati. So we put our money where our mouth is and posted up 3 metro buses with full top to bottom graphics.

The final production files came out to be some 50gb.

Festival Refreshes

It had been nearly 10 years since it's previous revisions, but it was time to step a bit further. For Beer Fest, I sincerely tried to craft a look without the gorilla but it just didn't feel right without him.

Wine Fest was another story, in my opinion, it needed some real work and a different direction. Wine Fest is our #1 selling and most expensive show, bringing in a more formal audience than other festivals.

Pickle Wars Festival

Pickle Wars was first introduced in 2022, around the time I started working at Jungle Jim's. This festival is wacky, it's fun and a little over the top. Guests are able to sample pickles and pickle flavored things and participate in the "Gherken Gauntlet" (pickle eating and brine drinking competition) to win a 12 inch gold wrestling belt.
